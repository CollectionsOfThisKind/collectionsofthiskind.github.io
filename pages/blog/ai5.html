The Lessons of Artificial Intelligence V: What's left for us then...

Once AI and robotics have taken everything from us, all that will be left to humanity is the questions with no answers; the cultural questions1 . AI can assess against ethical standards, but it can't make meta-ethical decisions for humans (that is, about the criteria for good and evil), because these are ultimately questions for humans.2

 

Notes:

1 In this, perhaps, the logical positivists (with the probable exception of Wittgenstein) were in error. Turing's seminal 1937 paper "On Computable Numbers..." to an extent sought to reveal the limits of computing by inventing it, and we've kind of been playing out that investigation ever since.

2 I'd say those humans affected, but that would be naïve.
