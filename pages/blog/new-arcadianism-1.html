The New Arcadianism I: For local people

Et In Arcadia Ego...

Anarchism is the new Arcadianism from people who have never lived in villages1.

 

Notes:

1 From Godwin onwards. Most of the early theorists of pure anarchism grew up in middle-sized towns, and those mixing anarchism and communism grew up on family estates. None of them actually grew up knowing what it was like to live in a community small enough that everyone could be involved in managing it, but large enough to supply the needed resources. This is equally true of all the anarchists I know now. I grew up in a village, and I can tell you there are pros and cons to their special kind of oppression. The first time I read Godwin, my thoughts just turned to my neighbours and what they thought everyone should be like – and probably, if allowed to, would have casually hanged people to make sure of. Great folk if you wanted to borrow a hedge-trimmer though.
