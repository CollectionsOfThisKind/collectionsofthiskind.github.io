The Lessons of Artificial Intelligence VI:
You are not who you think you are...

When people talk about intelligence being emergent, they usually mean individual intelligence emerging from the patterns of the brain; but if Large Language Models teach us anything, it is that individual intelligence is emergent from the patterns of society and predicated on society's use of language for exploration and storage1,2.

 

Notes:

1 And limited by our limited engagement with it.

2 Perhaps the strangest thing about LLMs is that despite being trained on the web, and that being largely Malinowskian Phatic communication, just by demanding it the intelligence trapped in these communications can be forced to rise to the top, probabilistically. On the face of it, you would imagine the swamp is too deep.
