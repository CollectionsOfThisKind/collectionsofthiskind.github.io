Notes:

1 Don't get me wrong; I live in a rural area, I know the natural strangeness of the place which one might describe, in short hand, as "folk horror"; but this is different from the modern desire of urban communities to escape to the countryside while self-hatingly imbuing it with an ironic, knowing and secret, danger.
