But retreat is death

I'm not well. When others aren't around, I get involuntary vocalisations; violent ticks; scream into the dark. I thought I could keep it together for appearance's sake, but it's starting to bleed into the real world: at an art group today, I found myself unable to stop squeezing my eyes tight shut and then flinging them open, repeatedly. The detritus bleeds out of me, and I can't stay clean; I have a lump developing in my neck; fungal infections that rot at my skin; something keeps eating away at my right shin. The PTSD is re-exerting itself, and I don't know why. I think it's the world. I drink constantly to keep it all at bay; but retreat is death.
