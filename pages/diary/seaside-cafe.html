Bleeding Real World

A first for me. I recently visited a seaside town, and I went into a Victorian-themed tourist café, only to find the whole thing seemed to have been hallucinated by an AI. It was a perfectly nice café, but you looked around and felt not only that the work on the walls was AI, but the design, feel, sense, of the place reeked of AI. It was like stepping into one of those slightly cartoony AI pictures people who haven't yet got the hang of prompting in detail generate. It was, as I say, a perfectly lovely café; indeed, was fun and had a nice vibe about it, but I did check I had the right number of fingers as I exited1.

25 July 2026

 

Notes:

1 Indeed, I haven't got out nearly enough. I was surprised to find the shops full of AI art, being sold for more than art by humans. You can easily pick up an engraved print edition of fishing boats by a good Edwardian artist for half the price of an AI computer print of a dolphin drinking a pint of beer. I suppose this is a good thing for those of us collecting Edwardian prints...
