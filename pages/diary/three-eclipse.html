Three Things: Horned Diana

1 Reading Falkener on the temple of Diana at Ephesus,
as the sun eclipses1 over the Old Man of Coniston,
and we await the Perseids, drunk on wine.

12 Aug 2026

 

Notes:

1 A partial eclipse, but enough to turn the sun to a pair of golden horns. I made the full eclipse in 1999, and though it was overcast it was extraordinary; took an almost empty train to Devon (people had been warned the trains would be chaos); watched from the top of Dartmoor, the shadow of the moon sweeping towards us on an unimaginable scale. After it was over, it took actual effort to talk again.
