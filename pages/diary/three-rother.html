Three things: Days down river

"Believe me, my young friend, there is nothing – absolutely nothing – half so much worth doing as simply messing about in boats2."

Drifting down the Rother1 on a warm summer evening,
we watched the minnows peck the gravel,
between the crowfoot and cress; one eye out for a bivvy spot.

5th August 1986

 

Notes:

1 The West Sussex Rother was our go-to haunt, water wise, away from the coast. Three or four of us kids would take a Canadian and drift lazily from where would could get minimum draught down to the Arun over a couple of days, take blankets and a tin pan and sleep around a bankside fire as we felt.

2 Kenneth Grahame, of course. It's a great book, but honestly, I'd have been happy all day with a book about Ratty and Mole mooching around on a crystal-clear chalk stream looking at caddis larvae and water boatmen. And Pan.
