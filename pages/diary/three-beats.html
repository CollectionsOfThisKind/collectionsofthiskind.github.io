Three things: Beats

In the bath
reading Anderson on Cassady1,
pushing the limits of my swelling liver.

Oh Vodka God2, protect me.

29 Jan 2026

 

Notes:

1 I think on my marginal youthful encounters with the Beats: seeing The Scroll somewhere, sometime, absolutely hung-over to hell; an embarrassed smile at Ferlinghetti in City Lights, too shy to go talk to him; chatting poetry with Seidenberg, last of the Beat booksellers, over a halfway decent bottle of Malbec ...and then, hanging around art shows hoping to bump into Carolyn, in Bracknell – unexpected final destination of a generation. I should have made more of an effort – as they rightly showed, youth is really no excuse.

2 The Vodka God is a nail-studded fetish I found in a market while passing through South Africa on the way back from working the Southern Ocean. It was after an insane night's drinking that involved, in unrelated ways: a terribly dead Maasai, narking off Michael Jackson, and a machine gunning. We were all a bit worse for wear by the morning and there it was, grinning at me from a junk stall like it knew I needed it. About a month later I was diagnosed with cirrhosis, and I dedicated the Vodka God to looking after me, feeding it Danzka and Havanas to ensure my fortunes. It's so far given me a good 25 years more life than I was expecting, so I won't hear from anyone that magic don't work.
