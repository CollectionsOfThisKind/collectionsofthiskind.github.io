Three things: School days

Walking to school1
through the cathedral quad,
And then, by the cottage gardens, lavender and bees.

5th June 1986

 

Notes:

1 I despised my school; I lived 10 miles away, so everyone else seemed to know each other, and the place thought it was a independent school because it was single sex: it was actually a pretty average comprehensive, but if you weren't in the A-team for one of the sports, you were nothing. It was the 80s, so also packed with Young Conservatives. I often cycled to school, but when I didn't, I'd get off the bus at the old market cross in the centre of the town, and walk the mile or so in, through the covered cathedral quad, down a row of small cottages, and through the old town gate, following south the old Roman street lined with small Georgian shops: booksellers, fishmongers, butchers: the smell of the medieval in the warm summer air. This, most days, was enough.
