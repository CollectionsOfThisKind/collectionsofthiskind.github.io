Three things: New Orleans

Passed upcycled garden shrines,
and rara echoing from white tin churches,
following the cut-loose, looking for graveyard dirt1,2.

17 May 2015

 

Notes:

1 Made a small detour on the way to a conference in Dallas to visit New Orleans, and was lucky enough to stay with a young AirB&B hostess who had a single-story in Tremé. Spent the nights dancing with her and her friends, and the days searching out what's left of voodoo outside of the museums and tat shops.

2 I have all the dirts: dirt from a New Orleans grave, dust from under a gallows; chalk from the Hellfire Caves. You never know when this stuff will prove useful.
